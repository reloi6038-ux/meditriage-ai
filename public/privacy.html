Privacy policy

Exactly what data MediTriage AI collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. No tracking, no ad tech, no PII by default.

✓ In one paragraph

We collect only what we need to run a triage assessment: age group, optional sex, symptoms, optional vitals, and an optional account email. We do not collect names, addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, device fingerprints, or any health record identifiers. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel, no ad network, no external AI service. Everything runs on this server and your device. You can delete your assessments and account from inside the app at any time.

🔒 Storage & security

Where data lives

Assessment records and account records are stored in MongoDB Atlas, with encryption at rest and TLS in transit. The server is hosted on Render (or your own infrastructure if you self-host this open-source project).

How we protect it

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (10 rounds) — the plain password is never stored or logged.
  • Reset tokens are hashed with SHA-256 before storage — the raw token exists only in the email we send you.
  • Session tokens (JWT) are signed with a secret that is unique per deployment, expire in 7 days, and are not stored server-side.
  • Input validation uses Joi with strict schema; unknown fields are dropped.
  • Rate limiting is applied to auth and assessment endpoints.
  • Security headers are set via Helmet (X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, etc.).
Bug bounty: If you find a security vulnerability, please report it privately via the GitHub repository's Security tab rather than opening a public issue.

👥 Who can access your data

Access is strictly scoped by role. There is no "support can see everything" backdoor.

A Anonymous users

Assessments are stored on your device only (localStorage). Nothing is sent to our server beyond the triage result you see on screen. Reference lookup works via the reference code, which you must know.

U Signed-in users

You can see only your own assessments. You cannot see other users' data. You can delete any of your assessments or your entire account from the History page.

Ad Administrators

Admins can see aggregate statistics, the list of users (email + display name only), and the audit log. They cannot see individual users' assessment content unless a specific support investigation is warranted — and every admin action is recorded in the audit log.

3 Third parties

None. No analytics, no CDN, no ad network, no LLM provider, no third-party logging. Everything runs on our server and your device.

📧 Email

What we send

  • Password reset emails — only when you request one from /reset.html. Contains your reset link and token, valid for 60 minutes.
  • That's it. No marketing, no newsletters, no "we miss you" emails.

How we send it

Reset emails are delivered via SMTP using a transactional email provider (SendGrid, Mailgun, Postmark, AWS SES, or Gmail). Your email address is shared with that provider only for the purpose of delivering the message. No tracking pixels, no read receipts, no click tracking.

⚖️ Your rights

You can exercise these rights directly inside the app or through the API — no email support required.

V

View everything we store about you

Signed-in users can view all their assessments on the History page → "My account" tab. For full export of raw data, use GET /api/v1/assessments with your JWT.

D

Delete individual assessments

On the History page, each assessment has a Remove button (local) or Delete button (server) that permanently removes it.

X

Delete your entire account

Issue DELETE /api/v1/auth/account with your JWT. This permanently deletes your account and all your assessments. This action cannot be undone.

O

Object to processing

You can use the app fully anonymously — no account required. Assessment data stays on your device only. This is the strictest form of opt-out we can offer.

P

Portability

Every result page has a Raw JSON button that downloads your full assessment in machine-readable JSON. Use this to export your data anywhere.

GDPR / CCPA: If you're in the EU, UK, California, Brazil, or another jurisdiction with data-protection laws, these rights are your statutory rights, not just a company policy. The endpoints above are the technical implementation.

🍪 Cookies & local storage

No cookies

This app does not set any HTTP cookies. No Set-Cookie headers, no session cookies, no tracking cookies.

What we use instead (localStorage)

Your browser's localStorage stores four small pieces of data, all scoped to this domain and all under your control:

  • mt-history — your assessment history on this device (up to 100 entries; delete any entry at any time)
  • mt-token — your sign-in JWT (only if you signed in)
  • mt-user — your email and display name (only if you signed in)
  • mt-theme — your light/dark mode preference

You can clear all of these by clearing site data in your browser settings. The app will still work — you'll just be signed out and your local history will be reset.
