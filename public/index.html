How the engine works

1

Structured intake

12 WHO danger signs, 24 symptoms, full vitals, hydration signs, pregnancy status, and chronic conditions — validated on both client and server.

2

Rules run first

Deterministic clinical rules outrank everything. A single WHO danger sign forces an EMERGENCY result before any statistical layer runs.

3

Bounded ML calibration

A logistic-regression model refines borderline cases — clamped so it can never escalate mild cases to emergency or downgrade an emergency.

4

Explainable result

Every result lists its reasons, factors, watch-for signs, action, timeframe, and a printable PDF. Non-diagnostic AI summary included.

Built for real clinics

WHO-grade intake

12 WHO/IMCI danger signs, 24 symptoms, full vitals (T°, HR, RR, SpO₂, BP, glucose, AVPU, pain), plus hydration & perfusion signs.

Emergency always wins

Any WHO danger sign forces an EMERGENCY result before any statistical layer runs. The ML calibrator can never downgrade or escalate beyond one rank.

Explainable results

Every result lists the exact reasons, main factors, action, timeframe, and watch-for signs — plus a probability breakdown.

AI, safely bounded

A deterministic, non-diagnostic AI summary explains the result, flags missing information, and suggests follow-up questions. It never diagnoses.

PDF export & reference

Every assessment produces a reference like MTA-4F7A-9B3C and a print-ready PDF clinical report. Raw JSON export for EMRs.

Admin-managed rules

Clinicians can add, edit, disable, and version clinical rules with priorities. Every change is logged in the audit trail.

! Clinical safety is not an afterthought

Every architectural decision prioritizes catching emergencies, avoiding false reassurance, and keeping the clinician in the loop.
